# World Happiness Report 2024

## Project Overview
This project analyzes the World Happiness Report 2024 using Google Sheets. It presents happiness scores across countries and explores the relationship between happiness, GDP per capita, and social support.

## Objectives
- Identify the top 10 happiest countries.
- Identify the 10 least happy countries.
- Analyze the relationship between GDP per capita and happiness.
- Analyze the relationship between social support and happiness.

## Tools Used
- Google Sheets
- Data Visualization
- Scatter Charts

## Key Findings
- Finland ranks first in happiness in 2024.
- Afghanistan has the lowest happiness score in the dataset.
- Countries with higher GDP per capita generally tend to have higher happiness scores.
- Countries with stronger social support generally tend to have higher happiness scores.

## Conclusion
The analysis suggests that economic conditions and social relationships are associated with happiness levels across countries.
